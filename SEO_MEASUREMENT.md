## Content plan
- Focus on visual discovery and actual artwork descriptions rather than further batches of city keyword pages.
- Collect confirmed artwork titles, medium, size, year, price and availability from the artist before adding purchasable listings.
- Write genuine studio/journal posts that include original photos, local exhibitions and stories of work being made, not mass-produced AI filler.
- Approach local spaces with a small curated catalogue rather than writing an unrelated page for every type of venue.
- After four to eight weeks, compare impressions per page and real contact enquiries; rewrite or prune pages with little unique value.
- Use a privacy-conscious analytics configuration, keeping consent obligations and policy in sync.

# Artwork descriptions: review record

Updated 2026-11-14. Every description in `src/data/artwork.mjs` was written after inspecting the original images in this repository, not by guessing from filenames. There are 32 distinct gallery works, described with short concrete alt text and user-visible notes on `/artwork-guide/`.

## Editorial rules

- Do not invent titles, medium, dimensions, pricing, people’s identities, editions, release dates or purchase availability.
- Describe the visible scene. Keep alt text succinct; captions are for readers. Do not stuff place names or 'watercolour' into every image.
- Where a photo is a *photo of an artwork*, describe the painted subject rather than the chair, mat or background unless the frame itself is meaningful.
- Use British English. No em dashes, stock 'beautiful moments' filler or fictitious emotions.
- The source files stay untouched. The guide loads them in the original gallery order.
- Descriptions may be updated after the artist confirms title, medium, dimensions, availability and any restrictions.
- The original homepage galleries keep their layout, and their lightbox descriptions / the gallery-guide link make descriptions discoverable without crowding the visual composition.
